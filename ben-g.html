What this build includes

  • Helmet: a thicker shell with a teardrop tail, vents you can cut through or paint, and straps that hang from the rim either side of each ear.
  • Stunner: the can loader is a round drum and the fizz can loads into the back.
  • Hands: a flat palm with knuckles, longer jointed fingers and a thumb at the side.
  • Runners are about a size and a half smaller, with a collar that covers the ankle.
  • Jeans keep both the front stripe and the side seams. Cheeks are off.
  • The backpack straps loop under the arms. The wrist computer has no aerial.
  • Run: heel strike, roll, toe-off and a real flight phase. Climb: the hips and shoulders sway and the knees turn out past the ladder.
  • Pogo: his spine and chest squash and stretch with the spring.
  • Eyes move: they follow the wrist computer, the phone, the ladder and the ledge, or you can aim them.
  • Idles: wrist check, helmet tip, head scratch with the helmet hanging from his hand, rocking on his heels, spinning the helmet on a finger and pulling out the wrist computer's aerial.
  • Pogo draw: he pulls the stick from his back pocket, or from the backpack when he wears it, and plants it.
  • Reload: he slides the spent can out of the back of the Stunner, drops it, takes a fresh one and pushes it home.
  • The dramatic death plays its beats: belly clutch, soles to the camera, dizzy wobble and the final topple.
