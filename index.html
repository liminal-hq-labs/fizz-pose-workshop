LIEUTENANT FIZZ / ART WORKSHOP

Classic Fizz, in motion.

The original native three-quarter Ben expanded into a complete pose study: the original flat-topped green helmet, broken white-and-green stripe, grey helmet rim, white EGA face, gold Fizz emblem, cyan denim seams and red high-tops. Running uses a 24 × 44 canvas; pogoing uses 24 × 52 and firing uses 32 × 44. The overworld uses a separate top-down 24 × 24 design.

Right-facing run · twelve ordered phases

Contact → roll-through → push-off → toe-off → flight → reach, then the opposite leg repeats. Elbows stay bent, the Stunner follows the near thigh, and the shoulders twist with the stride, and tiny eye and cheek shifts suggest the head turning towards and away from the camera. Each frame lasts 50 ms.

1. Near contact

Near foot lands; far heel is tucked. Near arm back, far arm forward.

2. Near roll-through

Near foot rolls flat under the hips; far knee swings through.

3. Near push-off

Near heel lifts into toe-off; far knee drives forward. Arms reverse.

4. Near toe-off

Near toe leaves the ground; far leg reaches forward.

5. Flight · far leg leads

Both feet airborne; far leg reaches ahead. Near arm forward, far arm back.

6. Far leg reaches

Far heel drops towards the ground; near heel kicks up behind.

7. Far contact

Far foot lands; near heel is tucked. Near arm forward, far arm back.

8. Far roll-through

Far foot rolls flat under the hips; near knee swings through.

9. Far push-off

Far heel lifts into toe-off; near knee drives forward. Arms reverse.

10. Far toe-off

Far toe leaves the ground; near leg reaches forward.

11. Flight · near leg leads

Both feet airborne; near leg reaches ahead. Near arm back, far arm forward.

12. Near leg reaches

Near heel drops towards the ground; far heel kicks up behind.
